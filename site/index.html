muster

Every device here can prove it belongs.

Device enrollment and identity for one household. A device makes its own key, you authorize one enrollment, and it carries a short-lived certificate that renews itself. The certificate is the membership — nothing to toggle, nothing to drift.

quadseven/muster how it works
Keys never move

Devices generate their own and send only a signing request. muster holds none of them.

One human decision

Six digits read off two screens, or a provisioning QR nobody has to touch.

Expiry, not a flag

Lapsing is the default end state. Revoking is a decision, refused on the next request.

Fine with muster down

Enrollment may need the internet. Operation must not: enroll at home, work anywhere.
